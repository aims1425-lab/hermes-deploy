# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
sex, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Respectful, constructive communication
- Focusing on issues, not personalities
- Welcoming different viewpoints and experiences
- Acknowledging and correcting mistakes

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, discrimination, and hostile language
- Personal attacks or public shaming
- Publishing private information without consent
- Sustained disruptive behavior in discussions

## Enforcement

Project maintainers will address reported violations by moderating comments, requesting edits,
or removing contributors from specific channels. Repeated violations may result in a
temporary or permanent ban from participation.

## Reporting

Report concerns through the repository issue tracker with details and context. Include links,
dates, and relevant messages so we can review quickly.
